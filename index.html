Setup

SportBike sets the speed bar to 0–60 and shows metres climbed. Ski sets it to 0–80, shows vertical descended, and counts lift rides on the summary. Both climb and descent are always saved.
Jump detectionReads the accelerometer for freefall. Needs the phone clamped firmly to the bars.
Jump sensitivityStart on Med and check the count against what you actually hit. Low catches only clean airs. High catches small doubles but chatter can fool it.
Minimum airAnything shorter is treated as trail chatter, not a jump.
UnitsSpeed and distance
Keep screen onHolds a wake lock while recording.
Keep recording in the backgroundHolds the app alive when you lock the screen or take a call, so Android can't kill the ride. Costs a little battery. Your ride is saved either way — this just keeps GPS running through the gap.
Refresh rateFast reads best on a jump run. Eco polls GPS half as often and roughly halves location power draw.
Daylight themeBlack is dimmer and cheaper on an OLED screen. Switch to light only if you can't read it in direct sun.
Rail top speedFull scale of the speed bar. Set it just above your usual top speed.
Accuracy cutoffFixes worse than this are ignored for distance.
Export everythingOne JSON file with every ride and jump. Keep a copy before you clear the app's data.
Air testLive accelerometer. Shake the phone or hop the bike with this open — the bar should drop into the green zone on every float. If your jumps are not registering, this tells you why.
